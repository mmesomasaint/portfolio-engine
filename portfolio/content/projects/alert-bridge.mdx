### Problem & System Constraints
During cascading outages, microservices generate thousands of identical alert webhooks simultaneously, leading to rate limiting and alert fatigue.

### Architectural Solution
Alert Bridge acts as an intelligent intermediary. It aggregates, deduplicates, transforms, and dispatches incident webhooks across multiple notification channels with rate-limiting and jittered exponential retries.
